# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained by one person, but anyone who participates is expected to help keep it respectful and constructive.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- Giving feedback about the work rather than making it personal.
- Being patient and assuming good intent while clarifying disagreements.
- Keeping discussions relevant to the website, its content, and its code.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Please report conduct concerns privately through the repository's available GitHub contact or security-reporting channels. Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer and handled as confidentially as practical.
